# Note Narrator – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 September 2026_

Note Narrator ("the app") is developed by Fearghus Kennedy as part of a University of Glasgow project. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

## What we collect

**Sheet music you upload.** When you upload a PDF or image, or take a photo of sheet music, that file is sent to the Note Narrator server so it can be converted into a talking score and audio. The server also stores the files it generates from it (MusicXML, MIDI and audio).

We do **not** collect:

- your name, email address or any account details (the app has no accounts)
- your location
- contacts, device identifiers or advertising identifiers
- analytics or usage tracking data

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services.

## How your files are used

Uploaded files are used only to produce the talking score and audio you asked for. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. They are not linked to your identity.

## Storage and retention

Uploaded and generated files are stored on the Note Narrator server and may be kept for troubleshooting and improving the service. You can ask for your files to be deleted at any time using the contact details below.

Please avoid uploading images that contain personal information other than the sheet music itself.

## Camera and photo access

The app asks for camera or photo library access only so you can choose or photograph sheet music. The app only ever accesses the images you select.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
